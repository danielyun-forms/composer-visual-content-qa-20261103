What was tested

Browser automation sent requests to hosted Composer and proposals were applied to dedicated unpublished QA forms. Saved Designer controls and desktop/mobile rendering were inspected. A Composer success message alone does not establish a pass.

Verdicts: Pass means the requested saved configuration or refusal was verified in the UI. Fail means a requested edit was rejected or saved incorrectly. Partial means only part of the expectation was verified. Blocked means an account prerequisite prevented the test. These are not blanket end-to-end passes.

The Onsite Browser Kit was installed and verified on the test storefront using Chrome for Testing 153.0.8010.12 and the observed hosted onsite revision 690310ccb3bdea3ce3e207a4b71031209662e776. Fixture delivery, document markers, script delivery, and a fresh identity passed.

Runtime limit: fresh draft preview links returned HTTP 404 with an expired-preview message. Checks requiring shopper execution, profile writes, or complete navigation are explicitly qualified. No forms were published and no real subscriber data was used.

Findings to investigate
  • Zero-step deletion was accepted and left the editor unable to render until the draft was restored.
  • Go to URL retained Submit form semantics; date format, age gate, and initial SMS consent produced unrecognized values. The SMS enum was repaired after clarification.
  • New wheel slice + success step, wheel probabilities, and coupon addition were rejected.
  • Desktop-only side image was stored as a background image visible on both devices.
  • Partial coverage: step reachability, reordered navigation, shared wheel target, past countdown, and Reviews content. Smart Opt-in requires an account prerequisite.

Drafts retained for inspection: XBMzK8 (general edits), XHCzWD (spin-to-win), RJ48MS (back-in-stock), S7hUqi (landing page). Drafts contain intentional test states and are not ready to publish.
